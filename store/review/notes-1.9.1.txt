What changed in 1.9.1: two crashes and one panel, plus what 1.9.0 never reached the store with.

• Fixed: on iPad (and an unfolded iPhone Duo), tapping "Agree & Start Creating" at the end of the welcome screens closed the app; the main screen was laid out before its detail column existed. It now opens, and so does "Skip". Verified on a fresh install on the iPad simulator, three launches of three.
• Fixed: the options panel under the prompt bar (Standard / Max) opens at its full height, not its 36-point grabber.
• From 1.9.0: ideas for your photo. A picked photo gets a row of three edits that fit it; tapping one puts it in the prompt bar. Ideas are included in the Pixie Week and in Plus. Nothing is sent before the person agrees: the row says a small copy of the photo goes to Anthropic's Claude, which writes the ideas, and that Pixie's servers keep no copy; tapping "Suggest 3 Edits" is the agreement. Settings > Photo Ideas turns it off; withdrawing the AI consent withdraws it too.
• From 1.9.0: saving an image to Photos no longer closed the app (it did on every tap of "Save"). It works with add-only and full photo access; Command-S uses the same path.

No account or sign-in is required; the app starts anonymously. Where region rules do not allow the models (China mainland, Hong Kong, Macau, Russia and other provider-excluded countries) the app says so in one neutral line instead of the prompt bar; the photo and gallery still work.

Prices (US), unchanged from 1.8.0: Plus is a weekly auto-renewing subscription at $6.99 a week, 60 edits a week, no free trial. Packs: 10 edits $2.99, 40 edits $9.99, 120 edits $24.99. An image costs 1 edit on Standard, 5 on Max. Photo ideas cost nothing.

Pixie Week: the first successful edit starts it; 5 Standard edits a day are free for 7 days, decided by our server. A device new to PixiePocket starts its week on its first edit, so it will be in day 1. A device that made an edit while reviewing an earlier version keeps that week: one that first edited while reviewing 1.9.0 is in it for 7 days from then; one that reviewed 1.8.0 or 1.8.1 in early November has finished it, so its edits come from Plus or a pack and the ideas row shows "See Plus".

How to test (iPad or iPhone, fresh install):
1. Finish the three welcome pages. The last explains the Pixie Week and asks for consent to send prompts and photos to Google Gemini and OpenAI (Guideline 5.1.2(i)). Tapping "Agree & Start Creating" opens the main screen — the crash fixed in 1.9.1; please try it on an iPad.
2. Tap "Try a sample photo" (or "Choose a photo"; picking needs no photo-library permission). Under the photo, "Suggest 3 Edits" sits above the sharing line. Tap it to agree; three ideas arrive in seconds. Tap one, then the arrow, to make the edit.
3. Or tap any "Popular edits" card (for example "Pro headshot") and choose a photo. The result arrives in about 15-40 seconds. During the Pixie Week the line under the prompt bar reads "Free today · N left"; after the first edit an alert announces the week, and "Remind me before it ends" asks for notifications and schedules one reminder two days before the end.
4. Tap the sliders button: the options open at full height with Standard (1 edit) and Max (5) — the panel fixed in 1.9.1.
5. Under the result, "Save" saves to Photos (first save asks for permission), "Share" shares it and "Compare" shares a before and after.
6. "Edits" at the top right opens the store: Plus preselected, the three packs below, the renewal terms (weekly, cancel in Settings), the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use on the same screen.
7. Settings > Photo Ideas switches ideas off and on.
8. The event link pixie://preset/holiday opens the Holiday card edit and its photo picker.

Images are private to each user; there is no public gallery. Every request passes provider moderation, and a declined or failed request never costs an edit. Sign in with Apple is optional and only syncs images and edits across devices.
