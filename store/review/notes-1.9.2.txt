What changed in 1.9.2: the Settings switches work again, iPad navigation in Settings, and ideas for people outside the Pixie Week.

• Fixed: Settings > Photo Ideas and Settings > Cloud AI Consent ignored taps on the switch itself (the row took the touch). Tapping the switch, or anywhere on its row, now flips it; Cloud AI Consent still asks before withdrawing. Checked on iPad and iPhone.
• Fixed: on iPad, Settings opened from the sidebar had no title, and Help and About had no Back button. Both are back. The API Connection check shows a normal checkmark instead of a stretched one.
• New: after the Pixie Week, someone without Plus still gets one idea per picked photo. Under the photo, "Suggest an Edit" (the same agreement as before) asks; the row then shows the first idea beside "More with Plus", which opens the store. Plus shows all three, and ideas cost nothing.
• New: Settings > About > Usage Counts. One switch decides whether the app sends anonymous usage counts, crash cards and daily performance numbers (MetricKit). None carries an identifier; TestFlight and development builds send no crash cards. The App Privacy label lists Crash Data and Performance Data, not linked, for App Functionality.

No account or sign-in is required; the app starts anonymously. Where region rules do not allow the models (China mainland, Hong Kong, Macau, Russia and other provider-excluded countries) the app says so in one neutral line instead of the prompt bar; the photo and gallery still work.

Prices (US), unchanged from 1.8.0: Plus is a weekly auto-renewing subscription at $6.99 a week, 60 edits a week, no free trial. Packs: 10 edits $2.99, 40 edits $9.99, 120 edits $24.99. An image costs 1 edit on Standard, 5 on Max. Photo ideas cost nothing.

Pixie Week: the first successful edit starts it; 5 Standard edits a day are free for 7 days, decided by our server. A device new to PixiePocket starts its week on its first edit, so it will be in day 1. A device that first edited while reviewing 1.9.1 (November 19 or 20) is still in its week until November 26 or 27 and sees "Suggest 3 Edits". A device that reviewed 1.9.0 or earlier has finished its week: its edits come from Plus or a pack, and under a picked photo it sees "Suggest an Edit", the new path in 1.9.2.

How to test (iPad or iPhone, fresh install):
1. Finish the three welcome pages. The last explains the Pixie Week and asks for consent to send prompts and photos to Google Gemini and OpenAI (Guideline 5.1.2(i)); "Agree & Start Creating" opens the main screen.
2. Open Settings (sidebar on iPad, the gear in the top bar on iPhone). Tap the Photo Ideas switch: it turns on. Tap it again: off. Tap Cloud AI Consent: an alert offers to withdraw; Cancel keeps it. Help and About open with a Back button.
3. Settings > About > Usage Counts shows what the app sends and the switch that stops it.
4. Back on the main screen, tap "Try a sample photo" (or "Choose a photo"; picking needs no photo-library permission). Under the photo, "Suggest 3 Edits" (in the Pixie Week) or "Suggest an Edit" (after it) sits above the sharing line. Tap it to agree; ideas arrive in seconds. Tap one, then the arrow, to make the edit.
5. Or tap any "Popular edits" card (for example "Pro headshot") and choose a photo. The result arrives in about 15-40 seconds. Under it, "Save" saves to Photos, "Share" shares it and "Compare" shares a before and after.
6. "Edits" at the top right opens the store: Plus preselected, the three packs below, the renewal terms (weekly, cancel in Settings), the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use on the same screen.

Images are private to each user; there is no public gallery. Every request passes provider moderation, and a declined or failed request never costs an edit. Sign in with Apple is optional and only syncs images and edits across devices.
